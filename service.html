Vital Wellbeing

Health Insurance Solutions

Health emergencies shouldn't jeopardize your life savings. Comprehensive healthcare plans safeguard you and your loved ones from inpatient hospitalization charges, surgeon fees, prescription regimens, and specialized outpatient rehabilitation.

  • Hospitalization & ICU: Direct cashless settlement across network clinics.
  • Individual vs. Family Floater: Consolidate family sum-insured limits under one umbrella.
  • Waiting Periods: 30-day initial illness exclusion, with 24-month pre-existing terms.
  • Common Exclusions: Cosmetic therapies, non-prescription supplements, and unapproved trials.

Family Security

Life Insurance & Income Protection

Life insurance acts as an indispensable economic shock absorber for your dependents. In the event of the breadwinner's premature passing, a pure term insurance policy ensures outstanding debts, mortgage obligations, and children's higher education are fully funded.

  • Pure Term Insurance: High coverage multiples with minimal recurring premiums.
  • Designated Beneficiaries: Direct legal payouts to named primary and contingent loved ones.
  • Policy Tenure: Align policy duration with active income-generating career years.
  • Honest Disclosure: Full medical history declaration prevents claims contestability.
On-Road Safety

Motor & Auto Insurance

Modern driving entails navigating congested highways and erratic conditions. Motor insurance protects against legal third-party damage claims while reimbursing repairs resulting from collisions, vandalism, theft, or weather hazards.

  • Third-Party Liability: Mandatory statutory protection against bodily injury and property loss.
  • Own-Damage Protection: Restores collision, fire, flood, and weather-inflicted damages.
  • Zero-Depreciation Rider: 100% reimbursement on replaced body panels and plastics.
  • No Claim Bonus (NCB): Cumulative discounts of up to 50% for accident-free driving records.

Property Defense

Home & Property Insurance

Your residence represents decades of diligence and family memories. Comprehensive homeowners insurance shields against structural catastrophes such as fires, windstorms, and burst pipes, while protecting personal belongings from theft and burglary.

  • Structure Rebuilding: Cost-to-rebuild protection based on local construction indices.
  • Valuable Contents: Itemized endorsements for electronics, jewelry, and furniture.
  • Alternate Accommodation: Living allowances if your home becomes temporarily unlivable.
  • Personal Liability: Indemnifies bodily injury sustained by visiting guests on property.
Global Exploration

Travel Insurance & Evacuation

Exploring international destinations should be exciting, not anxiety-inducing. International travel insurance guarantees immediate coordination and reimbursement for sudden hospital admissions, emergency medical repatriation, flight cancellations, and lost luggage.

  • Medical Evacuation: Critical airlift to accredited medical centers when local facilities fall short.
  • Trip Interruption: Reimburses non-refundable bookings due to sudden illness or bereavement.
  • Luggage Delay: Emergency purchase allowances for clothes and toiletries if bags are delayed >12 hours.
  • Destination Restrictions: Verify countries under active government travel warnings before departing.

Enterprise Longevity

Business & Commercial Liability

Running an enterprise involves multifaceted risks. Whether you operate a retail shop, professional consultancy, or manufacturing plant, commercial insurance safeguards physical assets, protects against customer injury claims, and keeps payroll solvent during operational halts.

  • Public Liability: Legal defense fees and indemnity if visitors slip or sustain injury on site.
  • Professional Indemnity: Protects consultancies from claims alleging negligence or errors.
  • Business Interruption: Replaces lost net revenue while premises undergo disaster reconstruction.
  • Electronic Equipment: Covers critical computing hardware, servers, and data restoration.
