Jargon Buster

Essential Insurance Terminology Decoded

Demystifying complex policy language. Here are six crucial insurance terms every consumer and policyholder should understand before signing or filing a claim.

Cost Sharing

Deductible vs. Co-Payment

A Deductible is the upfront fixed sum you pay out-of-pocket before insurance benefits start. A Co-Payment is a fixed percentage (e.g., 10%–20%) of each eligible medical or repair invoice you share with the insurer.

Underwriter Tip: Higher deductibles lower monthly premiums, but require maintaining readily available liquid emergency cash reserves.
Carrier Solvency

Incurred Claim Ratio (ICR)

The proportion of total premium revenue paid out in settled claims. An ICR between 70% and 90% reflects an insurer that is financially stable, solvent, and claim-friendly toward its policyholders.

Underwriter Tip: ICRs above 100% reflect carrier underwriting deficits; below 60% may suggest unusually strict claim rejection filters.
Motor Add-On

Zero-Depreciation Endorsement

Commonly termed bumper-to-bumper protection, this rider waives standard 30%–50% depreciation deductions on plastic, rubber, fiber, and metal vehicle components during accident repairs.

Underwriter Tip: Strongly recommended for all vehicles under 5 years old to guarantee 100% parts replacement reimbursement.
Health Clause

Waiting Periods (Health Plans)

A policy inception moratorium. The initial 30 days cover only accidental emergencies. Specific illnesses carry 1–2 year waiting, while Pre-Existing Diseases (PED) typically require 2–3 years continuous coverage.

Underwriter Tip: Never let your health policy lapse; switching insurers via portability preserves your accrued waiting credits.
Claim Modality

Cashless Network vs. Reimbursement

Cashless claims are settled directly between your insurer and network empanelled hospitals or workshops. Reimbursement requires you to pay upfront at non-network facilities and submit original bills within 15 days.

Underwriter Tip: Check that your local regional hospitals are on your carrier's current Tier-1 cashless network register.
Life Calculation

Human Life Value (HLV) & Cover

The calculated economic present value of your future income committed to dependent family members throughout your working life, minus your personal consumption costs and outstanding liabilities.

Underwriter Tip: The gold standard recommendation is securing pure term life insurance with a sum insured equal to 10x–15x your annual gross income.
Transparent Process

The Claim Resolution Roadmap: Step-by-Step

How an insurance claim actually moves from initial notification to complete cashless disbursement, with tips to avoid common administrative delays.

Stage 01

First Notice of Loss (FNOL)

Notify your insurer within 24 to 48 hours of an incident via the online portal or 24/7 hotline. Receive your official docket Claim ID immediately.

Stage 02

Digital Evidence Filing

Transmit digital scans of medical diagnosis sheets, police FIRs for vehicular incidents, or incident photos. Empanelled TPAs verify initial eligibility.

Stage 03

Survey & Loss Audit

An independent licensed surveyor or hospital medical auditor evaluates property damage or treatment charts to calculate reimbursable item totals.

Stage 04

Settlement & Release

Approved funds disburse directly to the cashless network facility, or wire straight into your linked bank account with a detailed settlement breakdown.
